# LIZ Video Assistant

LIZ has grounded knowledge for Zenith's Video Engagement OS, Smart Video Journey Engine, Patient Influence Engine, and Video Intelligence Center.

## Knowledge Coverage

- Video Engagement OS
- Smart Video Journey Engine
- Patient Influence Engine
- Treatment Acceptance Accelerator
- Attention scores
- Relationship health
- Revenue attribution
- Journey-first video mapping
- Provider video library

## Capabilities

- Answer video intelligence questions from the grounded knowledge base
- Recommend the Video Intelligence Center
- Explain attention scores, relationship health, treatment readiness, and attribution
- Recommend next-best video journeys from patient context
- Surface ALICE video recommendations as clickable actions

## Clickable Actions

- Open Video Intelligence
- Launch Video Campaign
- Launch Treatment Video Journey
- Launch Recall Journey
- Launch Membership Video Journey
- Launch Review Video
- Launch Referral Video
- View Video Analytics
- View Attention Scores
- View Revenue Attribution
- Recommend Next Best Journey

No plain-text-only video recommendations are required for supported intents. Every recommendation should include a problem, impact, recommended action, and launch or navigation button.
