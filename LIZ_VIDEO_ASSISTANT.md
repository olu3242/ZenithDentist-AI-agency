# LIZ Video Assistant

LIZ now understands Zenith's Smart Video Journey and Patient Influence model.

Added capabilities:

- Answer video intelligence questions from the grounded knowledge base
- Recommend Video Intelligence Center
- Launch treatment video journeys
- Launch membership video journeys
- Launch review video journeys
- Launch referral video journeys
- Explain attention scores and attribution

Clickable action examples:

- Open Video Intelligence
- Launch Treatment Video Journey
- Launch Membership Video Journey
- Launch Review Video
- Launch Referral Video

No plain-text-only video recommendations are required for the supported intents.
