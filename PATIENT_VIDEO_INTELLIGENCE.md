# Patient Video Intelligence

The Patient Video Intelligence layer tracks how patients engage with videos and converts that engagement into operating signals.

Tables:

- `video_deliveries`
- `video_engagement_events`
- `behavioral_signals`
- `engagement_patterns`
- `conversion_profiles`

Tracked signals:

- Video open
- Video completion
- Rewatch
- CTA click
- Portal login
- Appointment confirmation
- Treatment scheduling
- Review submission
- Referral submission
- Membership enrollment

Signals feed attention score, relationship score, retention risk, membership eligibility, readiness scores, and next-best-action recommendations.
