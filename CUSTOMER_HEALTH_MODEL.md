# Customer Health Model

## Inputs

| Dimension | Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation progress | implementation projects/tasks | Certified |
| Go-live readiness | go-live checklists, implementation scorecard | Certified |
| Adoption | implementation adoption metrics | Partial |
| Workflow usage | Workflow OS runtime health | Certified |
| Revenue proof | ROI proof and attribution records | Partial |
| PMS health | PMS operations surfaces | Partial |
| Incident/SLA posture | enterprise operations evidence | Certified |
| Expansion score | commercial intelligence and client health rollups | Partial |
| Churn risk | commercial intelligence and churn scores | Partial |

## Health Bands

| Score | Health |
| ---: | --- |
| 85-100 | Healthy / expansion-ready |
| 70-84 | Healthy with watch items |
| 50-69 | At risk |
| 0-49 | Critical |

## First Customer Health Certification

Model exists. Production health cannot be certified until one customer has implementation, adoption, revenue, PMS, and success review records.
