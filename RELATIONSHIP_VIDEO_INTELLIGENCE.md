# Relationship Video Intelligence

Video Engagement Score:

- Open: 10
- 50% Viewed: 15
- 90% Viewed: 25
- CTA Click: 35
- Appointment Confirmed: 50

Implemented helpers:

- `videoEngagementScore`
- `relationshipHealthScore`

Relationship Health Score includes:

- Review Activity
- Referral Activity
- Visit Consistency
- Video Engagement

Scores persist in `patient_video_scores`.
