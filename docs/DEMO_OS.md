# Demo OS

## Overview

Demo OS is the standardized 30-minute framework for demonstrating the Zenith platform to prospective dental practice clients. A successful Zenith demo is not a feature tour — it is a revenue discovery session that makes the prospect feel the pain of their current lost revenue and see Zenith as the obvious recovery system.

**Demo goal:** The prospect must leave the call with three things clear:
1. **Revenue At Risk** — How much revenue they are currently losing
2. **Recovery Automation** — How Zenith automates the recovery
3. **Attribution Proof** — How they will know it worked

---

## Demo Environment

Use the designated demo organization in the Zenith platform seeded with realistic data:

- Practice: "Sunrise Family Dentistry" (fictional)
- Providers: Dr. Sarah Chen, Dr. Marcus Webb
- Patients: 850 active (seeded with realistic influence scores, recall dates, treatment plans)
- Revenue Opportunities: $18,400 identified
- Revenue Recovered MTD: $4,200
- Reviews: 9 this month (network avg: 12)
- Acceptance Rate: 66% (network avg: 70%)

Always run the demo from the demo org — never from a real client account.

---

## 30-Minute Demo Framework

### Segment 1: Revenue Opportunities (5 minutes)

**Opening line:**
> "Before I show you anything, I want to find out what this practice is worth to you right now. Let me run a quick scan."

**Actions:**
1. Open Executive Dashboard → Revenue Opportunities panel.
2. Show the "Revenue At Risk" total — highlight the big number immediately.
3. Click into the top 3 opportunities (treatment follow-up, recall, membership).
4. For each: show patient name (demo data), amount at risk, and days since last contact.

**Talking points:**
- "These are patients who were in your chair. They said yes to care. Then life got in the way."
- "Without a system, these opportunities expire. The patient goes to a competitor or just doesn't come back."
- "We found [$X] in 10 seconds. ALICE scanned your PMS data automatically."

**Demo close line:**
> "This practice has [$18,400] in recoverable revenue we identified in 10 seconds."

---

### Segment 2: Digital Dentist Twin (8 minutes)

**Objective:** Show how ALICE delivers recovery via the provider's avatar — not generic text blasts.

**Actions:**
1. Open Avatar Studio → show Dr. Sarah Chen's avatar.
2. Play a 60-second avatar video (recall campaign script).
3. Show the script selector: recall / treatment follow-up / membership / referral / review.
4. Show the personalization layer: "Hi [Patient Name], I wanted to personally follow up..."
5. Show the delivery channel selector: SMS / Email / Voice.

**Talking points:**
- "This video was created once. ALICE sends it personalized to each patient."
- "Patients hear from their actual doctor — not a call center."
- "Watch rates on these videos run 55–70% in our network. Compare that to a text blast."

**Key visual:** avatar video playing + phone preview side-by-side showing personalized SMS with video thumbnail.

---

### Segment 3: Executive Dashboard (5 minutes)

**Objective:** Show the practice owner's command center view — everything happening in one place.

**Actions:**
1. Open Executive Dashboard.
2. Walk through the 4 KPI cards: Revenue At Risk / Opportunities Found / Revenue Recovered MTD / Forecast 30-Day.
3. Show Growth Score (0–100) with trend arrow.
4. Show the radar chart: Practice vs Network on 5 benchmark dimensions.
5. Show the provider leaderboard (Dr. Chen vs Dr. Webb composite scores).

**Talking points:**
- "This is your practice as a business — not just a schedule."
- "You see Revenue At Risk every morning. It's not a mystery anymore."
- "The radar chart shows exactly where you're below network average — that's where ALICE focuses first."

---

### Segment 4: Patient Influence Engine (7 minutes)

**Objective:** Show how Zenith scores every patient and surfaces the highest-value actions.

**Actions:**
1. Open Patient Influence Scores → filter by score >75.
2. Show a sample high-score patient: influence score 82, treatment at risk $1,800, recall 23 days overdue.
3. Show AI Revenue Intelligence recommendations panel: 3 recommendations with estimated revenue impact and confidence.
4. Click "Activate" on the treatment follow-up recommendation — show journey activation flow.
5. Show the journey steps: SMS Day 1 → Video Day 3 → Follow-up call Day 7.

**Talking points:**
- "Every patient has a score. ALICE knows who's most likely to convert and acts on them first."
- "You're not sending 850 people the same message. You're sending the right message to the right person."
- "When you hit Activate, ALICE takes over. You don't have to think about this patient again unless they respond."

---

### Segment 5: Revenue Attribution (5 minutes)

**Objective:** Close by showing proof — ALICE's actions → patient booked → revenue confirmed.

**Actions:**
1. Open Revenue Attribution → show the attribution chain for a completed example.
2. Walk through: Opportunity Identified → Journey Activated → Patient Opened Video → Patient Booked → Revenue Confirmed.
3. Show the MTD attribution total and ROI calculation: "$4,200 recovered / $597 subscription = 7× ROI this month."
4. Show the forecast: "Based on your current pipeline, next 30 days = $6,800 projected."

**Talking points:**
- "This is your proof. Every dollar has a chain of custody from ALICE action to payment."
- "Most dental software can't tell you what worked. We can tell you exactly."
- "You started this call with $18,400 at risk. This is what happens when we close that gap."

## Demo Close Framework

**Final summary:**
> "Here's what you saw today: [X] in revenue you're currently losing. An AI system that finds it, sends personalized video from your doctor, and tracks every dollar back to the source. The question isn't whether this pays for itself — the math makes that obvious. The question is: how much longer do you want to leave [X] on the table?"

**Offer:**
> "We can have your practice live in 7–14 days. The first thing ALICE will do is run a real scan on your actual PMS data and show you what we find. That alone is worth the call."

**Close options:**
1. "Want to get started today? I can send the agreement in the next 10 minutes."
2. "Would it help to see your actual practice data? I can set up a data preview session — no commitment."
3. "Who else needs to be in the room to make this decision?" (loop in partner/office manager)

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|---------|
| "We already have a recall system." | "Great — so you're sending messages. The question is: are those messages from the doctor's avatar, personalized, tracked back to revenue? What's your current recall rate? Network average is 65%. I can show you where you sit in 30 seconds." |
| "We're too small for this." | "Essentials starts at $297/month. Our smallest practices are solo dentists with 200 active patients recovering $2,000–$5,000 per month. The ROI math works at every size." |
| "We just signed with [competitor]." | "What are they doing about treatment follow-up attribution? Can they show you exactly which message caused a patient to book? That's the gap we fill. Happy to show you the side-by-side." |
| "I need to think about it." | "Totally fair. Here's what I'd suggest: let me send you the ROI calculator with your practice's rough numbers plugged in. Takes 60 seconds to fill out. If the number doesn't make sense, don't move forward. What's your average production per patient per visit?" |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | "We offer a 90-day performance review. If you haven't seen measurable revenue attribution in 90 days, we'll work with you until you do — or discuss your options. Our network average is 8–15× ROI. We want that number on your account, not just in our marketing." |

## Demo Checklist (Pre-Call)

- [ ] Demo org seeded with fresh data (Revenue At Risk > $15k)
- [ ] Avatar video rendered and playable (Dr. Chen recall video)
- [ ] All 5 dashboard segments loaded and ready
- [ ] Discovery notes reviewed: prospect's primary pain point identified
- [ ] Calculator ready: prospect's est. production × acceptance gap = revenue at risk estimate
- [ ] Screen share tested + recording consent confirmed (if recording)

---

## Post-Demo Actions (Within 2 Hours)

1. Log demo in `sales_activities` with outcome and next action date.
2. Send follow-up email with:
   - Demo recording link (if applicable)
   - ROI calculator pre-filled with their numbers
   - Proposal PDF (if Demo → Proposal signal was strong)
   - One-line next step: "I'll follow up [day] at [time] — does that work?"
3. Update `sales_pipeline` stage from `demo` to `proposal` or back-to-`demo` based on outcome.
