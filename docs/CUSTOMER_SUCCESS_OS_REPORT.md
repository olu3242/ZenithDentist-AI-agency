# Customer Success OS Report

Date: 2026-06-01

## Required Metrics

Implemented in `buildCustomerSuccessFramework`.

- Adoption Score
- Revenue Impact Score
- Workflow Health Score
- Automation Coverage Score
- Engagement Score

## Measurement

- Adoption Score: active users divided by expected users
- Revenue Impact Score: production lift versus baseline
- Workflow Health Score: healthy playbooks divided by installed playbooks
- Automation Coverage Score: installed playbooks divided by required PROS playbooks
- Engagement Score: blended product adoption and ALICE recommendation acceptance

## Status

Customer Success OS is operationally defined and compatible with existing `operations-core` health analytics.
