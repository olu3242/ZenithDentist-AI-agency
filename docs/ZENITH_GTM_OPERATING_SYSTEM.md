## System Overview

The Zenith GTM OS is the complete customer acquisition, sales, onboarding, and retention system for signing and retaining dental practice clients.

## Component Map

```
ACQUIRE
├── Organic Search (SEO + Content)
├── LinkedIn Outreach
├── Cold Email (3-touch sequence)
├── Google Ads
├── Referral Partners (dental CPAs, consultants)
├── Dental Associations
└── Conference Leads
        ↓
CONVERT
├── Homepage CTA (tracked)
├── Revenue Assessment (automated audit)
├── Audit Preview + Download
├── Strategy Session (Calendly)
└── Proposal + Close
        ↓
ONBOARD
├── Organization created
├── Portal access granted
├── PMS integration activated
├── Workflows configured
└── Welcome + onboarding call
        ↓
RETAIN
├── Monthly revenue recovered reports
├── LIZ Executive Intelligence briefings
├── 30/90/365 check-ins
├── Churn signal monitoring
└── Expansion / upsell conversations
        ↓
GROW
├── Case studies from retained clients
├── Referral requests at 90-day mark
├── Testimonials → homepage social proof
└── Repeat the cycle
```
