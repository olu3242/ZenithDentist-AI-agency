# Zenith Patient OS™ — Executive Deployment Guide

> For practice owners and decision-makers. What Zenith does, what gets deployed, and what to expect.

## What Is Zenith Patient OS™?

Zenith Patient OS™ is an AI-powered growth platform built specifically for dental practices. It runs in the background of your practice — connecting to your existing patient management system, learning from your patient behavior, and automatically sending the right message to the right patient at the right time. It turns your patient data into booked appointments and attributed revenue without requiring your staff to do extra work.

In 30 days, practices on Zenith typically see measurable revenue attribution from automated patient engagement, with a target return of 3x their subscription cost.

At Day 30, your Zenith CSM presents your first Executive Business Review (EBR):

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does this replace any of my staff?**
No. Zenith handles the repetitive communication tasks (reminders, follow-ups, review requests) so your team can focus on patient care and high-value conversations.

**Q: What if a patient doesn't want to receive these messages?**
Every message includes an opt-out mechanism. Opted-out patients are never contacted again through Zenith. Opt-out rates for well-configured practices are typically < 2%.

**Q: What if we already use a recall system?**
Zenith integrates with or replaces your existing recall system. In most cases, it significantly outperforms legacy recall tools because it uses AI to score and prioritize patients rather than sending blanket messages.

**Q: How is patient data protected?**
Zenith is HIPAA-compliant. All patient data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any data access. See `docs/HIPAA_COMPLIANCE_GUIDE.md` for full details.

**Q: What happens if we stop using Zenith?**
Your patient data remains in your PMS. Zenith does not retain patient records after contract termination. All communications stop immediately upon cancellation.
