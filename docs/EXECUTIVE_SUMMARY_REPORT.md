## ALICE Executive Intelligence — 7 Detection Capabilities

ALICE's executive layer detects and reports on:

1. Recall rate risk (revenue leakage from lapsed patients)
2. Workflow failure risk (automation reliability degradation)
3. Revenue forecast risk (confidence below threshold)
4. Treatment acceptance risk (case presentation opportunity)
5. High-value patient churn risk (influence score decline)
6. Commercial pipeline stall (proposals not advancing)
7. Competitive positioning gap (benchmarks vs peer practices)

**Intelligence score range:** 0–100. Target ≥80 (Excellent).
