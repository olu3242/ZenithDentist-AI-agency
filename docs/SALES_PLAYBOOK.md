# Sales Playbook

## Zenith Revenue Recovery — Sales OS

**Date:** 2026-06-03

---

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

**Primary:** Solo or 2-3 doctor dental practice
- Revenue: $800K – $3M/year
- Staff: 5-15 employees
- Pain: Feels like they're leaving money on the table but can't see where
- Tech: Using Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental (PMS)
- Decision maker: Practice owner/dentist

**Secondary:** DSO (Dental Service Organization) — 3-10 locations
- VP of Operations or Regional Director
- Standardization and visibility problem across locations

---

## Discovery Framework

### 5 Core Questions

1. **Revenue Visibility:** "If I asked you right now what percentage of your recall patients are 90+ days overdue, could you tell me without pulling a report?"

2. **Treatment Acceptance:** "What's your current treatment acceptance rate, and how do you track it across providers?"

3. **No-Show Impact:** "What does a no-show cost you on average, and do you have a system that automatically fills those slots?"

4. **Review Growth:** "How many new Google reviews did you get last month, and how did that compare to the month before?"

5. **Time Cost:** "How many hours per week does your team spend on tasks that feel like they should be automatic by now?"

**Qualification signal:** If they can't answer 3+ questions without uncertainty → they need Zenith.

---

## ROI Calculator Inputs (Assessment Form)

| Input | Why It Matters |
|-------|---------------|
| Number of chairs | Capacity baseline |
| Average visit value | Revenue per slot |
| Current no-show rate | Recovery opportunity |
| Recall gap rate | Reactivation opportunity |
| Monthly new patients | Growth velocity |
| Current review count | Reputation opportunity |

**Output:** Monthly and annual revenue recovery estimate, delivered as downloadable audit.

---

## Recovery Opportunity Presentation

Lead with the audit numbers:

> "Based on what you shared, your practice has **$[monthly_recovery]/month** in recoverable revenue sitting in three areas: **[top_3_recommendations]**. The question isn't whether the opportunity is there — it clearly is. The question is whether you want a system that captures it automatically."

---

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have software" | "Zenith isn't a replacement for your PMS — it's the revenue intelligence layer that sits on top and tells you what your PMS is missing." |
| "We're too busy to implement" | "Implementation takes 2 hours. We do it for you. Your team doesn't change their workflow." |
| "It's too expensive" | "If the audit shows $3,100/month recoverable and Zenith costs $[price]/month, what's the ROI conversation?" |
| "Let me think about it" | "What specifically are you weighing? I want to make sure the numbers we showed you are accurate for your situation." |

---

## Closing Framework

**Discovery → Proposal → Close in 14 days (target)**

1. **Strategy Session (30 min):** Review audit findings, confirm numbers, identify top 2-3 recovery opportunities
2. **Proposal (same day):** Customize ROI projection, present pricing, send agreement
3. **Follow-up (48h):** Address one specific objection, share a relevant case study
4. **Close:** Contract + setup fee invoice
