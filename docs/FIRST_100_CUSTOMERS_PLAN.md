## Months 1-3: Prove the Model (Clients 1-10)

**Strategy:** Manual, high-touch, founder-led sales

**Channels:**
1. Personal LinkedIn network (dental professionals known to founder)
2. Cold outreach to 200 practice owners (LinkedIn + email)
3. 1-2 dental study club presentations

**Targets:**
- 200 outreach contacts/month
- 30-50 assessment submissions/month
- 8-12 strategy sessions/month
- 3-5 new clients/month

**Key Activity:** Close first 10 clients personally. Document every objection. Refine the discovery script. Build 3 case studies.

---

## Months 4-6: Establish Process (Clients 11-25)

**Strategy:** Templatize what worked, add referral channel

**New Channels:**
- Referral partner program (dental CPAs, consultants)
- First dental association presentation (state dental society)
- 1-2 targeted Google Ad campaigns

**Process Additions:**
- SDR handles initial LinkedIn outreach
- Automated follow-up email sequences after assessment
- Formal partner referral tracking (partner_id in leads.source)

**Targets:**
- 5-8 new clients/month
- First 3 referral-sourced clients

---

## Months 7-9: Scale Acquisition (Clients 26-50)

**Strategy:** Multi-channel with performance tracking

**Channel Mix:**
- LinkedIn: 30% of leads
- Cold email: 25% of leads
- Referral partners: 20% of leads
- Google Ads: 15% of leads
- Dental associations/conferences: 10% of leads

**Team:** 1 full-time SDR + founder closing

**Targets:**
- 8-12 new clients/month
- CAC < $[TARGET]
- LTV > $[TARGET]

---

## Months 10-15: Scale to 100 (Clients 51-100)

**Strategy:** Account Executive model + CSM team

**Team:**
- 2 Account Executives
- 1 Customer Success Manager
- 1 SDR
- Founder → VP Sales oversight

**Automation:**
- Lead scoring from assessment data (practice health score as MQL signal)
- Automated proposal generation from audit data
- CSM health scoring triggers from portal engagement metrics

**Targets:**
- 10-15 new clients/month
- 95%+ retention at 90 days
- NPS 8+
- ARR: $[TARGET]
