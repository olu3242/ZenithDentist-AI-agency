**Date:** 2026-06-03

## Pre-Call Preparation

Review the prospect's audit before the call:
- `audits.liz_report` — full AI-generated opportunity analysis
- `roi_calculations` — all 5 opportunity dimensions
- `audits.recommendations` — top 6 recovery recommendations
- `audits.snapshot_90_day` — 90-day opportunity window

**Know these numbers cold before the call:**
- Monthly recovery estimate
- Practice Health Score
- Top 3 recommendations
- Annual pipeline value

---

## Call Structure (30 Minutes)

### Minutes 0-5: Open + Confirm Context

> "Thanks for taking the time. Before we dive in, quick question — when you filled out the assessment, was there anything in the numbers that surprised you?"

Listen for: shock (good — they didn't know), skepticism (address the data), excitement (lean in).

### Minutes 5-15: Walk the Audit

Share screen (or send PDF before call). Walk through:

1. **Practice Health Score** — contextualize against industry average
2. **Top Opportunity** — lead with the biggest dollar amount
3. **How Zenith Captures It** — connect each opportunity to a specific workflow
4. **90-Day Snapshot** — make it concrete: "If we started today, by [date] you'd have captured X"

### Minutes 15-25: Qualify + Surface Objections

> "Does this match what you're seeing day-to-day, or are there areas where the numbers feel off?"

Then: "If we could solve [top problem] reliably and automatically, is that something you'd want to move on in the next few weeks?"

### Minutes 25-30: Next Steps

If qualified → "I'll send the proposal today. It'll have your specific numbers, our implementation timeline, and the pricing. What's the best email for you?"

If not yet ready → "What would need to be true for this to be the right time?"

## Qualification Criteria (BANT+)

| Criterion | Green | Yellow | Red |
|-----------|-------|--------|-----|
| **Budget** | Can allocate $[price]/mo | Needs owner approval | Under financial stress |
| **Authority** | Owner/decision-maker on call | Influencer, owner not present | Gatekeeper only |
| **Need** | 3+ pain points confirmed | 1-2 pain points | Satisfied with status quo |
| **Timeline** | Wants to start within 30 days | 60-90 days | No urgency |
| **PMS** | Dentrix/Eaglesoft/Open Dental | Other supported PMS | Unsupported PMS |

---

## Red Flags (Do Not Proceed)

- Practice is for sale or owner is retiring within 12 months
- Office manager is resistant and owner is disengaged
- Practice is in severe financial distress (can't invest in growth tools)
- Expectation: "This will replace my front desk staff"

## Case Study Bank (Use During Call)

**Recovery archetype 1 — Recall Reactivation:**
> "We worked with a 3-chair practice in [state] who had 240 overdue hygiene patients. Within 90 days of Zenith's recall system, they had reactivated 67 of them. That was $41,200 in incremental revenue they weren't tracking before."

**Recovery archetype 2 — No-Show Reduction:**
> "Their no-show rate was 22%. After automated confirmation and same-day fill workflows, it dropped to 11%. That's 4-5 extra visits per week — roughly $1,400-1,800 in recovered chair time monthly."

**Recovery archetype 3 — Treatment Acceptance:**
> "Unscheduled treatment plans are almost always a follow-up problem. Zenith's automated treatment plan follow-up sequence increased their acceptance rate from 34% to 49% in 60 days."
