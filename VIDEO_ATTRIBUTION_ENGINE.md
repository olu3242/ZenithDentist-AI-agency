# Video Attribution Engine

Attribution tables:

- `journey_outcomes`
- `video_attribution_records`

Tracked revenue classes:

- Revenue influenced
- Revenue recovered
- Revenue protected

Mandatory write targets remain compatible with the wider production evidence layer:

- `workflow_execution_evidence`
- `revenue_attribution_records`
- `alice_recommendation_traces`
- `mission_control_outcomes`

The video-specific attribution table provides the journey and delivery-level proof needed to connect patient engagement to revenue outcomes.
