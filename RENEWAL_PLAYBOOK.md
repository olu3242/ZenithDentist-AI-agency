## Renewal Gates

- Client health reviewed.
- ROI proof generated.
- Workflow performance reviewed.
- Open incidents reviewed.
- Expansion opportunities reviewed.
- Contract terms confirmed.

## Certification Boundary

Renewal process is defined. A first renewal cannot be certified until a live customer reaches renewal date.
