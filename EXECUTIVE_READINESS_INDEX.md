# Executive Readiness Index

Readiness formula:

Evidence Coverage + Revenue Attribution Coverage + ALICE Traceability + Incident Coverage + Recovery Coverage + SLA Compliance

Levels:

- 0-59: FAIL
- 60-79: PARTIAL
- 80-94: READY
- 95-100: CERTIFIED

Implemented display surfaces:

- Executive Center
- Product Owner Center
- NOC
- Certification Center

The index is calculated by `calculateExecutiveReadinessIndex` and surfaced through `getEnterpriseOperationsState`.
