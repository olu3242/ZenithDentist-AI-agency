# ALICE Video Intelligence

ALICE determines:

- Best video
- Best sequence
- Best timing
- Best channel
- Best CTA
- Best follow-up
- Best conversion strategy

Inputs:

- Appointment type
- Treatment type
- Patient history
- Engagement history
- Attention score
- Relationship score
- Retention risk
- Membership eligibility

Outputs:

- Video recommendation
- Journey recommendation
- Next best action
- Expected outcome
- Expected revenue impact
- Confidence score

The `/portal/video` page surfaces ALICE video recommendations with problem, impact, action, workflow ID, and confidence.
