# Provider Video Library

Provider video library support is represented by:

- `provider_video_profiles`
- `video_library`
- `video_categories`
- `video_templates`

Supported provider video types:

- Welcome videos
- Procedure videos
- Recovery videos
- Review videos
- Referral videos
- Membership videos
- Success story videos
- Educational videos

Each video stores organization scope, category, provider profile, URL, transcript, thumbnail, language, version, status, tags, and metadata.
