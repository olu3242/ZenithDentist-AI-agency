# Mission Control Video Center

Implemented route:

- `/portal/video`

Widgets:

- Videos Sent
- Videos Viewed
- Completion Rate
- Attention Score
- Treatment Readiness
- Membership Readiness
- Reviews Generated
- Referrals Generated
- Revenue Influenced
- Revenue Recovered
- Revenue Protected

The page uses the shared portal shell/grid system and reads from live video intelligence tables when Supabase service credentials are configured.
