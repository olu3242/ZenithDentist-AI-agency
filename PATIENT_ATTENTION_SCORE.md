# Patient Attention Score

Patient Attention Score is stored through `behavioral_signals` and summarized through `engagement_patterns`.

Inputs:

- Video opens
- Video completion
- Portal usage
- Recall compliance
- Appointment compliance
- Review engagement
- Referral engagement
- Membership engagement

Output:

- Attention Score from 0 to 100

ALICE uses this signal to prioritize next-best-action recommendations and decide whether to educate, remind, recover, escalate, or attribute.
